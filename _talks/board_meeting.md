Two parallel sessions:
<p>
<b>EMLG/JMLG Board Meeting</b>
Participation in this session is limited to members of the Board of Directors of EMLG/JMLG and will take place in the main auditorium, immediately after the end of Session 3. Board members should remain in the auditorium after that session.

<p>
<b> Early-Career Networking Session</b>
This session will occur in parallel with the EMLG/JMLG Board meeting and is open to all PhD students and Post-docs registered at the conference. It is designed as an opportunity for Early-Career Researchers to network and discuss their research in an informal setting. This will take place in the TIC foyer, around the poster boards. Drinks will be provided.
